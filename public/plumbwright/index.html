About

The person you call is the person who shows up.

Michael Wright, owner

PlumbWright Plumbing & Gas, LLC is Michael Wright — an independent plumber who handles the call, the quote and the wrench himself. No dispatch queue, no upsell script, no second crew showing up with a different number.

That means straight answers about what actually needs replacing, a price you hear before the work starts, and an explanation of what went wrong so you can spot it yourself next time. Customers put it plainly in their reviews: on time, reasonably priced, knowledgeable, clean.

Homeowners, landlords and property managers all use PlumbWright the same way — for the 9pm emergency and for the scheduled project that has to be finished before the drywall crew arrives.

Questions

The things people ask on the first call.

Anything not covered here, ask on the phone. You'll get a yes or a no, not a maybe.

+ Who actually shows up?

Michael Wright, the owner. He takes the call, quotes the job and does the work — there's no dispatcher in between and no second crew arriving with different numbers.

+ Do you work weekends?

Saturday and Sunday are open around the clock. Weekdays run 3–10 PM. Those are the hours on the Google listing — for anything outside them, call and ask.

+ How soon can someone get out here?

It depends on the week, so the honest answer is to call and ask. Reviewers describe quick callbacks and next-day availability, including on active leaks.

+ Will I know the price before work starts?

Ask for the number up front and you'll get it. Customers who collected quotes elsewhere consistently describe PlumbWright's as the reasonable one — no prices are published here because every job is different.

+ Do you take rentals and property-management work?

Yes — several of the Google reviews come from managers and landlords using PlumbWright across multiple properties on a standing basis.

+ Will you explain what went wrong?

If you want to know, yes. It's the thing reviewers single out most often — what failed, why it needed replacing, and what to watch on the parts that were left alone.

+ Is the work done to code?

Yes, and customers say so in writing — clean, efficient and up to code is a direct quote from a repeat customer with work across several properties.
